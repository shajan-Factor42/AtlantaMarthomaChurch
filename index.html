Welcome

Welcome to Mar Thoma Church, Atlanta

We have been worshipping in Atlanta for more than forty years — first as the Atlanta Mar Thoma Church and Hermon Mar Thoma Church. In December 2018 the two congregations came together as one: Mar Thoma Church, Atlanta.

We welcome you in the name of our Lord and Savior, Jesus Christ, to come and worship with us.
