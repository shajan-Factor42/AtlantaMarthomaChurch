About us

Our story

Forty years in Atlanta

For more than forty years, Mar Thoma families in Atlanta have gathered to worship as the Atlanta Mar Thoma Church and the Hermon Mar Thoma Church. In December 2018 the two parishes merged to form one congregation: Mar Thoma Church, Atlanta.

Today we are a family of believers who worship in both Malayalam and English, raising the next generation in the faith while staying connected to our heritage.

Our heritage

The Malankara Mar Thoma Syrian Church traces its roots to the apostolic ministry of St. Thomas in India in the first century. It is a reformed Eastern church that holds together ancient liturgy and an evangelical faith centered on Scripture, and today has parishes around the world.
